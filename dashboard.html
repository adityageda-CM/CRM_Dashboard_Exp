What's working vs what's broken
Working
• Home page icon and merchandising together drive 70–80% of all TEA traffic
• CRM and organic user sets are 95.8% non-overlapping — no cannibalisation
• System 1 (DB qualifying) identifies 1,700–2,100 users/day correctly
• A/B: Simple messages outperform Personalised (2.0% vs 1.4% CTR)
• Total CRM reach is significant: 10k+ msgs/day at peak
Broken / needs fixing
• Deep link post-mpin: CRM gets 0.01% click-to-city-view rate (should be ~60%)
• System 2 pipeline: 63% of qualifying users never enter MoEngage flow
• 0% booking CVR: No post-click conversions in 13 days across all CRM channels
• Price mismatch: UX audit found consistent price inconsistency across verified experiences
• Unknown attribution: 19% of tea_city_view visits have unknown source
